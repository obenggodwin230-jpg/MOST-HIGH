Welcome

A House of Prayer for All People

Sabbat Church of God Most High is a congregation built on the foundation of Jesus Christ — dedicated to making the Lord known as the Divine Saviour and persuading all to become His disciples.

We are a Bible-believing, Sabbath-observing community. We gather each week to worship, pray, study the Word of God, and grow together in faith. All are welcome to come as they are and encounter the living God.

Our supreme aim is to teach the Truth of God faithfully, equipping every member to walk in obedience, love, and purpose according to the Scriptures.

"Make the Lord Jesus Christ known to all men as the Divine Saviour — persuade them to become disciples of Christ."
— Our Founding Purpose
Core Beliefs

What We Believe

📖

The Holy Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — our supreme authority for faith, doctrine, and godly living in every area of life.

✝️

Jesus Christ

We confess Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour — fully God, fully man — who died for our sins, rose from the dead, and will return in glory.

🕊️

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the Holy Spirit who convicts, converts, and dwells within every true believer, empowering a life of holiness and service.

🌙

The Holy Sabbath

We observe the seventh-day Sabbath as commanded by God — a sacred day of rest, worship, and communion with the Most High, set apart from the beginning of creation.

🙏

Repentance & Baptism

We preach repentance, faith in Christ, and baptism as the doorway into a new life — dead to sin and alive to God through Jesus Christ our Lord.

🌍

The Great Commission

We are called to go into all the world, making disciples of every nation — teaching them to observe all that Christ commanded us.

The Holy Sabbath

Why We Keep the Sabbath

From the very beginning, God hallowed the seventh day as a sign between Himself and His people. The Sabbath is not a burden — it is a blessing, a delight, and a foretaste of eternal rest in God Most High.

We observe the Sabbath from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday, following the pattern established at creation, confirmed in the Ten Commandments, and kept by Jesus Christ Himself during His earthly ministry.

📅

Seventh Day

Saturday — the biblical seventh day of the week, set apart by God at creation.

🌅

Sunset to Sunset

Observed from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset, as established in Genesis.

⛪

Corporate Worship

We gather as a congregation each Sabbath for worship, teaching, and fellowship.

📜

God's Commandment

"Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy." — Exodus 20:8
